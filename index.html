How to hedge

Long on one venue, short on the other, from the same address.

  1. One address for both venuesThe hedge only counts when both legs sit on the same L1 address.
  2. Pick a market listed on bothLook for volume on both sides and pair funding at or above zero.
  3. Open the first leg with a limit orderMaker volume is cheaper. Note the size in coins, not dollars.
  4. Open the opposite leg, same size in coinsThen paste your address above and check that delta is near zero.
  5. Keep a buffer2–3x leverage and at least 25% to liquidation. If a leg runs against you, add margin.
  6. Close both legs togetherNever leave one side open on its own.
No take-profit or stop-loss on a single leg. If it fires, the other leg is left unhedged. Close both manually.

Not allowed

  • Trading against yourself on one venue (main account vs sub-account or a second wallet). That is wash trading.
  • Splitting volume across sub-accounts to farm points.

Risks

  • Funding drifts. A pair that earns today can pay tomorrow.
  • Gaps. A fast move can liquidate one leg before you top it up.
  • Rules change. What counted last week may not count now.
