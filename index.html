How to hedge across RH and Core

  1. Use one address for both venues. The hedge only counts when both legs sit on the same L1 address. Not a second wallet, not a sub-account trick.
  2. Pick a market listed on both. The table above shows only markets active on both venues. Prefer ones with depth on both sides and pair funding at or above zero.
  3. Open the first leg with a limit order. Maker volume is cheaper and is understood to weigh more. Note the size in coins, not dollars.
  4. Open the opposite leg with the same size in coins. Prices differ slightly between venues; delta is measured in coins. Then paste your address here and check that the delta is close to zero.
  5. Keep a real buffer. 2–3x leverage on each leg and at least 25% to liquidation. If one leg runs against you, add margin to it instead of closing it.
  6. Close both legs together. Never leave one side open on its own.
No take-profit or stop-loss on a single leg. If it fires, the other leg is left naked with leverage. Close manually, both legs.

Not allowed

  • Trading with yourself on the same venue, for example long from the main account and short from a sub-account or a second wallet on RH. That is wash trading.
  • Splitting volume across sub-accounts to farm points.

A hedge between RH and Core is different: the two venues have separate order books, so each leg trades against real counterparties.

Risks this page will flag, but cannot remove

  • Funding drifts apart. RH and Core funding is set independently, and a pair that earns today can pay tomorrow.
  • A gap on one leg. A fast move can liquidate one side before you top it up.
  • Rules change weekly. What counted last week may not count this week.
